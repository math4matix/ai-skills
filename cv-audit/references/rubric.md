# Scoring rubric (100 points)

Score each line with an anchor, cite a quote. Juniors are judged against other juniors: school projects count as experience, but only when they show outcome, scope and tools.

## Content: 40
| Criterion | Pts | Full marks look like | Zero looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact & evidence | 15 | Bullets state what changed + a number or concrete outcome ("600+ users", "cut X from A to B") | Duty lists ("odpowiedzialny za…") |
| Role relevance | 10 | Top half matches the target role/ad; irrelevant items cut or shortened | Generic CV for any job |
| Technical/skill proof | 8 | Each skill backed by a project, with honest levels; stack named precisely | Skill list with no evidence; unexplained "intermediate" |
| Ownership & depth | 4 | Clear "I did" vs team; one non-trivial problem solved | Everything "team" or everything vague |
| Credibility | 3 | Claims verifiable (links, dates, named competitions) | Round, unverifiable claims |

## Structure: 20
| Criterion | Pts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Section order | 6 | Header → profile (2-3 lines) → what is strongest for this stage first (juniors: projects/education) → skills → extras |
| Length & density | 6 | Exactly one page; no wall of text; no padding |
| Scannability | 5 | Bullets 1-2 lines, parallel grammar, key terms early |
| Header & availability | 3 | Name, city, email, phone, GitHub/portfolio, availability dates |

## ATS: 20
| Criterion | Pts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Text layer & reading order | 8 | Selectable text; single column or clean order; no text in images |
| Standard headings & dates | 5 | Recognised section names; dates in one consistent, parseable format |
| Keyword coverage | 5 | Role keywords spelled as in job ads; with an ad, report must-have hits/misses |
| Safe formatting | 2 | No tables/icons/graphics carrying meaning; common fonts |

## Language: 20
| Criterion | Pts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Correctness | 8 | Typos, spelling, grammar, diacritics (count each distinct error; 0 errors = 8, 4+ = 0) |
| Consistency | 6 | One tense/voice/person across bullets; one language for skill levels and terms; consistent punctuation |
| Concision & tone | 4 | Professional, active, no fillers or clichés ("team player", "zaangażowany") |
| Terminology | 2 | Correct, standard tech terms |

## Adjustments (cap -15, itemise each)
- Polish legal/format norms (see `pl-norms.md`): up to -5
- Red flags: unexplained gap, inflated or unverifiable claim, inconsistency, security/ethics-sensitive claim framed carelessly: up to -5
- Links & contact: unprofessional email, dead/ambiguous links, GitHub with nothing to show, no portfolio when role expects one: up to -3
- Visual design: fonts, margins, spacing, hierarchy, whitespace: up to -3

## Verdict bands
85-100 advance to personal review · 70-84 borderline, fix first · below 70 likely filtered.
Any failed knockout caps the verdict at "borderline" regardless of score.
