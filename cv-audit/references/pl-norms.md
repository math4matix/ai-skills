# Polish CV conventions (students/juniors)

Apply these when the CV is in Polish or targets a Polish employer. Rules here are conventions and law-adjacent practice, not guarantees; word flagged items as "recommended", not "illegal".

- **RODO/GDPR clause.** For recruitment, include a consent line at the bottom, e.g. "Wyrażam zgodę na przetwarzanie moich danych osobowych w celu przeprowadzenia procesu rekrutacji. [Ew. także na przyszłe rekrutacje.]" (legal basis for the current process is the labour code, but employers' ATS forms expect the clause). Missing: -2 to -3. A clause listing the employer's name is fine; a clause that is outdated (references to the repealed 1997 act) is a -1 language/legal note.
- **Photo.** Optional. Never penalise absence. Penalise a low-quality or casual photo (-1 under visual design). Do not suggest adding one.
- **Personal data minimum.** Name, city, email, phone are enough. Flag home address, date of birth, PESEL, marital status as excess (-1 each, max -3). Never recommend adding them.
- **Phone.** Prefer `+48 600 000 000` grouping; accept `600-000-000`.
- **Email.** Name-based, no nicknames or jokes.
- **Dates.** One format, `MM.RRRR – MM.RRRR` or `RRRR – RRRR`. Include expected graduation year for students.
- **Student/intern CV.** Education can lead; list school, profile/kierunek, expected graduation, relevant achievements (średnia/stypendium/konkursy) and, for technikum, practical-training (praktyki) availability dates.
- **Verbs.** Polish CVs commonly use past-tense verbs ("Wdrożyłem…", "Zaprojektowałem…") or impersonal noun phrases ("Wdrożenie…"). Third-person ("Zastąpił…", "Współtworzy…") reads like a reference letter and mixes with first-person elsewhere; pick one form. Flag mixed tense (present "Współtworzy" next to past "Zastąpił") as inconsistency.
- **Language levels.** Use CEFR (A2/B1/B2/C1) for foreign languages; skill levels for tech should use one language (all Polish or all English) and consistent labels.
- **Names of awards.** Use official names ("Stypendium Prezesa Rady Ministrów" vs "Rady Ministrów"); if unsure, ask the user to check the certificate.
- **Common typos to scan for:** missing diacritics, spelling slips, English calques ("real-time" is acceptable; "deadline" ok), quote marks (use „…”).
